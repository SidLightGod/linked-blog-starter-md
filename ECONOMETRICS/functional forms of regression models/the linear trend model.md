$$ Y_t = B_1 + B_2t + u_t $$ this is, regress Y over time itself, where time is measure chronologically. Such a model is called, approximately, the linear trend model. **And the time variable t is known as the trend variable** 

- if the slope coefficient is positive there is a upward trend in Y however, if the slope coefficient is negative, there is a downward trend in Y.
