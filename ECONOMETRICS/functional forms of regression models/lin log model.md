#### the relationship between expenditure on services in relation to total personal consumption expenditure 
1. suppose we want to find out how expenditure on services behaves if total personal consumption expenditure increases by a certain percentage 
2. suppose we consider the following model $$ Y_t = B_1 + B_2lnX_{2t} + u_t $$
3. the change in log of a number is a relative change. therefore the slope coefficient in this model measures $$ B_2 = \frac{abs\delta{Y}}{relative\delta{X}} $$(the absolute change in Y over the relative change of X) $$ => \frac{\delta{Y}}{\delta{X}}{X} $$
4. in other words this implies that $$ \delta{Y} = B_2(\frac{\delta{X}}{X}) $$
5. this equation states that the absolute change in Y equal to B2 times the relative change in X. 

> lin-log models are used in situations that study the absolute change in dependent variable for a percentage change in the independent variable.
