the compound interest formula we know 
$$ Y_t = Y_0(1 + r)^t $$
Y0 - the beginning or initial value of Y 
Yt = Y value at time t 
r - the compound rate of growth of Y 

taking natural log we get 
$$ lnY_t = lnY_0 + tln(1 + r) $$
now we let : $B_1 = lnY_0$ 
and $B_2 = ln(1 + r)$

so we get the following 
$$ lnY_t = B_1 + B_2t$$
adding the error term we get the expression 
$$ lnY_t = B_1 + B_2t + u_t $$
> these are called the semi log model because only one variable appears in the logarithmic form

> for a semilog model, the slope measures the proportional or relative change in Y for a given absolute change in explanatory variable, time in the present case. If this relative change is multiplied by 100, we obtain the percentage change or growth rate **Because of this the semi log models and such models are routinely used to measure the growth rate of many variables, whether economic or not**
