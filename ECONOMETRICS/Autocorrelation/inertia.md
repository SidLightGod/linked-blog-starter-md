1. a distinguishing and distinct feature of most time series data in economics is the sluggishness or inertia. 
2. as is well known, time series such as GDP, employment, money supply, and price indexes exhibit business cycles. 
3. there is a momentum built into these time series and the upswing continues until something happens to slow down.
4. Therefore, in regression involving time series data, successive observations are likely to be correlated or interdependent.
