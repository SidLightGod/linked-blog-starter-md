1. the term autocorrelation can be defined as the correlation between numbers of observations ordered in time. 
2. autocorrelation is generally associated with time series data. However autocorrelation can also occur in cross sectional data in which case it is called spatial correlation. 
3. in context of CLRM, it is assumed that the correlation does not exist in the disturbance term $u_j$, symbolically, no autocorrelation means $E(u_i,u_j) = 0$ 
4. this implies that the expected value of the product of two error terms is zero. 
5. however if this is not the case and the product of two error terms is not equal to zero, this implies that autocorrelation exists.
