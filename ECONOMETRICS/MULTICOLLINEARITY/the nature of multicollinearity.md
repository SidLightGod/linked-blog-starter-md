>in the case of perfect collinearity or multicollinearity among the explanatory variables, we can not obtain unique estimates of all the parameters, and since we can not obtain unique estimates we can not draw any statistical inferences, about them from a given sample. 

> in other words, perfect multicollinearity happens when one independent variable in a regression model can perfectly predict the other independent variable in the same regression model, imperfect collinearity happens when there is a high degree of correlation between independent variables but not perfect.
