###### some points of concern about this test 
1. the error term in the regression suggested by Park may itself be heteroscedastic as well as serially correlated. 
2. Glejser however has maintained that in large samples, the models suggested by him hold well in detecting heteroscedasticity.
3. therefore Glejser can be used as a diagnostic tool in large samples.
