###### what is one appealing feature about this equal division
1. one appealing feature is that it is symmetric. each agent has the same bundle of goods and no agent prefers any other agent's bundle of goods to his or her own, since they all have exactly the same thing. 
2. unfortunately an equal division will not necessarily be pareto efficient. If the agents have different tastes they will desire to trade away from the equal division.
