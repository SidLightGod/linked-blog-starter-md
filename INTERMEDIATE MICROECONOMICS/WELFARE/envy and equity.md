1. we say that an allocation is equitable if no agents prefers any other agent's bundle of goods to his or her own. 
2. if some agent i prefers some other agent j's bundle then we say that i envies j. 
3. finally if an allocation is both equitable and pareto efficient, we will say that the allocation is a fair one.
