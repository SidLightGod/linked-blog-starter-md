1. in general, the optimal quantity of the public good will be different at different allocations of the private good. 
2. But if the preferences are quasilinear, it turns out that there will be a unique amount of public good supplied at every efficient allocation. 
3. the utility function takes the form of $u_i(x_i, G) = x_i + v_i(G)$. This implies that the ***marginal utility of the public good is always 1.***
