1. we can think of each person as having some endowment of a private good wi. Each person can spend some fraction of it on private good and some of it on the public good. 
2. we use x1 for the private good and g1 for the public good and suppose that the c(G) = G, which implies that the marginal cost of providing a unit of public good is constant at 1. 
3. the total amount of the public good provided will be g1 + g2 = G. since each person cares about the total amount of the public good provided the utility function is $u_i = (x_i, g_1 + g_2)$.
4. in order for the person 1 to decide how much to contribute to the public good he should have some forecast for how much person 2 will contribute. 
5. the simplest thing to do here is to adopt the Nash equilibrium. We assume that person 2 also makes some guess about person 1's contribution.
