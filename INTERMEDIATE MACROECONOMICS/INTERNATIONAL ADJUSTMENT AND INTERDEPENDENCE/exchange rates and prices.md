1. a devaluation that takes place when domestic and foreign prices are constant will succeed in reducing the relative price of a country's goods, and will thus improve the trade balance.
2. however, the price level typically changes along the exchange rate. the essential issue when a country devalues is whether it can achieve a real devaluation. 
3. a country achieves real devaluation when the price of the goods of its own country reduces relative to the price of the foreign goods.

10. however it has to be noted that the devaluation can be offset by an increase in the domestic prices. It is therefore important for a country with an external deficit to reduce its prices in dollars because if devaluation leads to a rise in the domestic prices then there is no gain competitiveness.
