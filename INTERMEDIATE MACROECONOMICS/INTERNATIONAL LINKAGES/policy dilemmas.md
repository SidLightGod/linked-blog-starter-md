1. the potential for the capital flows to finance a current account deficit is extremely important. 
2. countries frequently face policy dilemmas, in which a policy is designed to deal with one problem worsens another problem 
3. In particular, there is sometimes a conflict between the goals of external and internal trade balance 
4. the external balance is when the balance of payments is close to balance. Otherwise the central bank is either losing reserves - which it can not keep on doing - or - gaining reserves, which it does not want to do forever.
5. internal balance exists when the output is at the full employment level. In addition, countries want to maintain internal balance, or full employment.

11. the presence of interest sensitive capital suggest a solution to the dilemma : if the country can find a way of raising the interest rate, it would obtain financing for the trade deficit. 
12. this implies that both the monetary and the fiscal policy would be used to achieve external and internal balance simultaneously.
