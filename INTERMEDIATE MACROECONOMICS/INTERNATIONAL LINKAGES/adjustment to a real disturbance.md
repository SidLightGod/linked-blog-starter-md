2. At the initial interest rate, exchange rate, and output level, demand for our goods now exceeds the available supply 
3. for goods market equilibrium at the initial rate of interest we require higher level of output. Accordingly, the IS schedule shifts out and to the right, to IS'. 
4. now consider for a moment point E', at which the goods and money markets clear. Here the output has increased to meet the increased demand. The rise in income has increased the money demand and thus raised the equilibrium rate of interests.
