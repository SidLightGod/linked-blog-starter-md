1. in a fixed exchange rate system the central bank stand ready to buy and sell their currencies at a fixed price in terms of some other currency. 
2. the major countries had fixed exchange rate system from the end of world war 2 until 1973. 

# INTERVENTION 
1. foreign central banks hold reserves - inventories of dollars and gold that they can sell for dollars - to sell when they want to or have to intervene in foreign exchange market. 
2. intervention is the buying or selling of foreign exchange by the central bank. 
3. given the market demand and supply, the price fixer has to make up the excess demand or take up the excess supply. 
4. so as long as the central bank has the necessary reserves, it can continue to intervene in the foreign exchange market to keep the exchange rate constant. 
5. However if a country persistently runs deficits in the balance of payments, the central bank will eventually run out of foreign exchange reserves and will be unable to continue to its intervention. 

###### the amount of intervention that is needed? 
1. the balance of payments measures the amount of foreign exchange intervention required from the central banks.
