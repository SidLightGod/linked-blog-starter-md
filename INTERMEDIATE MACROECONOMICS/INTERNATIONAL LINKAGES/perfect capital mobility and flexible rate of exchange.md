1. in this section we use the Mundell - Fleming model to explore how monetary and fiscal policy works in an economy that has fully flexible exchange rates and perfect capital mobility 
2. under the fully flexible exchange rates the central bank does not intervene in the market for foreign exchange. The exchange rate must adjust to clear the market so that the demand for and supply of foreign exchange balance. 
3. without central bank intervention, therefore, the balance of payments must equal to zero. 
4. under fully flexible exchange rate the absence of intervention implies a zero balance of payments. 
5. any current account deficit must be financed by private capital inflow; a current account surplus is balanced by capital outflows. 
6. adjustment in the capital account ensures that the sum of current and capital account is zero. 
7. the second implication of the fully flexible rate of exchange is that the central bank can set the money supply at will. 
8. since there is no obligation to intervene there is no link between the money supply and the balance of payments
9. perfect capital mobility implies that there is only one interest rate at which the balance of payments will balance 
10. at any other rate of interest the balance of payments is so large that the balance of payments can not be zero.
