1. our working assumption from now on is that the capital is perfectly mobile.
2. capital is perfectly mobile when the investors can purchase the assets in any country they choose, quickly, with low transactions cost, and in unlimited accounts. 
3. when capital is mobile, asset holders are willing and able to move large accounts of funds across borders in search of the highest return or lowest borrowing cost. 
4. the high degree of capital integration implies that any one country's interest rates can not get too far out of line without bringing about capital flows that tend to restore yields to the world level. 

###### the recognition that interest rates affect capital flows and the balance of payments has important implications for the stabilization policy? 
1. first, because monetary and fiscal policies affect interest rates, the policies have an effect on the capital account and therefore on the balance of payments. 
2. the effects of the monetary and fiscal policies on the balance of payments are not limited to the trade balance effects 
3. the second implication is that the way in which the monetary and fiscal policies work in affecting the domestic economy and the balance of payments changes when there are international capital flows
