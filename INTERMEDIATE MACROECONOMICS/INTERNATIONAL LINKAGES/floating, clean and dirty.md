1. in a system of clean floating, the central bank completely stands aside and allow exchange rates to be freely determine the foreign exchange markets. 
2. since the central banks do not intervene in this system, official reserve transactions are, accordingly zero. 
3. That means the balance of payments is zero in a system of clean floating : the exchange rate adjusts to make the current and capital accounts sum to zero. 
4. under managed floating or dirty floating, central bank intervene to buy and sell foreign currencies in attempts to influence exchange rates. 
5. official reserve transactions are accordingly not equal to zero under managed floating. 

###### terminology 
1. a devaluation takes place when the price of foreign currencies under a fixed rate regime is increased by official reserve action. 
2. a devaluation thus means that the foreigner pay less for the devalued currency or that the residents of the devaluing country pay more for foreign currencies. The opposite of devaluation is revaluation.
3. changes in the price of foreign exchange rates are referred to as currency depreciation and appreciation. 
4. a currency depreciates when, under floating rates, it becomes less expensive in terms of foreign currencies.
