the balance of payments is the record of transactions of the residents of a country with the rest of the world. 

1. the current account records the trades in goods and services as well as transfer payments and services include freight, royalty payments, and interest payments. Services also include net investment income, the interest and profits on our assets abroad less the income foreigners earn on assets they own in United States. 
2. services also include net investment income which is the interest and profit on our assets abroad minus the income foreigners earn on assets they own in the US. 
3. transfer payments include remittances, gifts and grants. The trade balance simply records the trade in goods. 
4. the simple rule for the balance of trade accounting is that any item that increases the payment by a country's resident is a deficit item in that country's balance of payments. 
5. the current account is in surplus if the export of the goods exceed the imports plus the net transfers to the foreigners, that is, if receipts from trade in goods and services and transfers exceed the payments on this account 
6. the capital account records the purchases and sale of assets such as stocks bonds and land. there is a capital account surplus also called a net capital inflow.
