1. in an interdependent world our policy changes affect other countries as well as ourselves, and then feed back on our economy. 
2. when we increase government spending, our income increases, part of our rise in the income will be spent on the imports, which means that the income will rise abroad too. 
3. the increase in foreign income will then raise the foreign demand for our goods, which in turn adds to the domestic income expansion brought about by high government spending, and so on. 
4. repercussion effects also arise in response to the exchange rate changes.
