1. inflation is the rate of change of prices which means that the price level today reflects the pattern of past inflations. 
2. unless the inflation is negative, which is called deflation, the price level does not fall. 
3. inflation can be expressed as $$ \pi \equiv \frac{P - P_{-1} }{P_{-1} } $$
4. where the P_-1 is the last period's price level and P is the current price level.
