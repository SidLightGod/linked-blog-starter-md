adaptive expectation for inflation can be defined as follows : $$ \pi^E = \pi_{-1}^E + a(\pi_{-1} - \pi_{-1} ^E) $$
where a is a positive constant whose value is less than or equal to 1. If past forecast mistakes are fully corrected so that the a = 1, we would have $\pi^E = \pi_{-1}$ 

- this form of adaptive expectation therefore provides an interpretation of the lagged inflation term, as a determinant of inflation. 
- the inflation inertia can be explained as the tendency of the inflation rate to persist
- inertia augmented Phillips Curve $$ \pi = \pi_{-1} + \alpha(y - y_e) $$
- where pi - 1 is the inflation inertia 
- expectations augmented Phillips curve $$ \pi = \pi^E + \alpha(y - y_e) $$
- the y - ye is basically the output gap in both the cases.
