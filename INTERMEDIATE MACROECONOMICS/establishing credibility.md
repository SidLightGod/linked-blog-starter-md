###### what are some better ways of dealing with the time inconsistency
1. the first step could be to make the central bank independent. An independent central bank implies that the decisions regarding the interest rate and the money supply can be made by the central bank independent of the influence of the currently elected politicians. 
2. the second step is to give the central bank an incentive to take the long view. that is to take into account the long run cost of increased inflation. 
3. one way of doing this is to give them long terms in office so, they have a long horizon and have incentives to build credibility. 
4. the third step may be to appoint a "conservative" central banker who dislikes inflation a lot and is therefore less willing to accept a higher inflation in exchange of lower unemployment. 
5. when the economy is at the natural rate, the banker would be less willing to embark on a monetary expansion, thus the problem of time inconsistency will be reduced.
