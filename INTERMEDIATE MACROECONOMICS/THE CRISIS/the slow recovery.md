
###### why has the recovery been slow in US? 
1. some economist argue that the banking crisis has changed the natural level of output, so it would be wrong to think that we can go back to the pre crisis level of output. 
2. more accurately, taking into the account that the output grows over time, it would be wrong to think that the output can return to its pre crisis level. 
3. the weak recovery that we see is probably the best the economy can do. 

###### why the output remains below its old pre crisis trend? 
1. the banking crisis may affect the efficiency of the bank system for a long time which can lower the productivity 
2. regulations which involve increasing the capital ratio of the bank does indeed decrease the risk but they also make the intermediation between the borrower and the lender costly and thus decreasing the natural level of output. 
3. in a typical recovery, monetary and fiscal policy can be used to hasten the return to the natural level of output, however in the current crisis they can play only a limited role at best. 
4. there is no room for conventional monetary policy and limited scope for unconventional monetary policy. The worries about the debt is putting pressure on the government to reduce the deficit, to pursue fiscal consolidation instead of fiscal expansion
