###### measures which were aimed at strengthening the financial system 
1. in order to prevent a run by depositors, federal deposit insurance was increased from 100k to 250k, the funds of the banks did not come much from the deposits but from the issuance of short term debt to investors. 
2. In order to allow the banks to continue funding themselves, the federal government offered a program guaranteeing new debt issues by banks. 
3. the federal reserve provided widespread liquidity to financial system. if the investors wanted some of their funds back the banks had no option but to sell their assets, often at the fire sale prices. In most cases, this would have meant bankruptcy. 
4. to avoid this, the fed put in place a number of liquidity facilities. It allowed not only banks but also other financial institutions as well to borrow from the Fed
5. finally it increased the set of assets that the bank can use as collateral when borrowing from the fed. these facilities allowed the banks and financial institutions to pay back investors without having to sell their assets. 
6. given the importance of the housing sector in the crisis, Fed bought mortgage backed securities, and remained the main buyer of these securities for years to come. 

###### the troubled assets relief program or TARP? 
1. the government introduced a program called the TARP aimed at cleaning up the banks 
2. the initial goal of the $700 billion program, introduced in October of 2008, was to remove the complex assets from the balance sheet of the banks, thus decreasing uncertainty, reassuring investors and making it easier to assess health of each bank. 
3. the treasury however faced the same problems as the private investors. if these complex assets were to be exchanged for say treasury bills, at what price should the exchange take place, is an important question to be asked. 
4. within few weeks it was realized that the task of assessing the value of the assets was a difficult task 
5. the new goal became to increase the capital of the banks. This was done by the government by acquiring shares and thus providing funds to the most of the largest US banks. 
6. by increasing their capital ratio and decreasing their leverage ratio, the goal of the program was to allow the banks to avoid bankruptcy and over time return to the normal 


###### the fiscal policy approach 
1. when the Obama administration assumed office in 2009, its first priority was to design a fiscal policy that would increase the demand and reduce the size of recession. 
2. such a fiscal program called the American Recovery and Reinvestment act was passed in Feb 2009. 
3. it called for 780 billion dollar in new measures, in form of tax reductions and spending increases., over 2009 and 2010.
