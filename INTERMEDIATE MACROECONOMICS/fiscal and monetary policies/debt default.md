1. at some point, when a government finds itself facing very high interest rates, it may decide to default. 
2. Default is often partial and creditors take what is known as a haircut. 
3. a haircut of 30% implies that the creditors get only 70% of what they were owed. 
4. it maybe unilaterally imposed by the government or it may be the result of a negotiation with creditors. creditors knowing that they will not be fully repaid in any case may prefer to work out a deal with the government 
5. when debt is very high haircut might seem like an appealing solution. having a lower level of debt after default reduces the size of required fiscal consolidation and thus make it more credible.
6. it lowers the required taxes, potentially allowing for high growth. but default comes with a very high cost. 
7. if for example the debt is held by the pension funds, the retirees may suffer very much from the default. If it is held by banks then banks may go bankrupt, with major adverse effects on the economy. 
8. if the debt is held by foreigners then the country's reputation may be lost and it may be very difficult for the government to borrow abroad for a long time. 
9. so in general, governments are very reluctant to default on their debt.
