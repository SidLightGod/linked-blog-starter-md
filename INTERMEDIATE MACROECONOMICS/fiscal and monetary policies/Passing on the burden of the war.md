wars lead to an increase in spending and it is important to consider the implications of financing this increase in spending through debt or increased tax. 

==**we begin our analysis by first assuming the output at the natural level.**== 

#### through deficit financing 
1. suppose that the government relies on deficit financing with the sharp increase in the government spending, there will be a large increase in the demand for goods. 
2. ==given our assumption that the output remains same, the interest will have increase enough so as to maintain equilibrium. Investment, which depends on the interest rate, will decrease sharply.== 

#### through an increase in taxes 
1. suppose the government decides to finance the spending increase through an increase in taxes - say income taxes. Consumption will decline sharply. 
2. the longer the consumers expect the war to last, the longer they will expect the higher taxes to last, and more will they decrease their consumption. 
3. **in any case, the increase in government spending will be partly off set by a decrease in consumption**

> in short, for a given output, the increase in government spending requires either a decrease in the consumption or decrease in the investment. 

> **the more the government relies on deficit, the smaller the decrease in the consumption during war and the larger the decrease in investment. Lower investment implies a lower capital stock after war, therefore lower output after war. By reducing capital accumulation, deficits become a way of passing some of the burden of war onto future generations.**
