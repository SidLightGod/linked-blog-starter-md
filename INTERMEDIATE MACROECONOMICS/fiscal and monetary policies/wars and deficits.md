1. wars typically bring about large budget deficits. two largest deficits in the US economy took place during world war 1 and world war 2. 

###### two reasons for running deficits during war 
1. the first is distributional : deficit finance is a way to pass some of the burden of the war to those who are alive after war, it seems only fair for the future generation to share in the sacrifices the war requires 
2. the second is more narrowly economic : deficit spending helps reduce tax distortions.
