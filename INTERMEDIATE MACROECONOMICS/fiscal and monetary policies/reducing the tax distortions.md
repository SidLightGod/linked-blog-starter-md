1. there is another argument for running deficits, not only during wars but more generally, in times when the government spending is exceptionally high. 
2. the argument is as follows if the government were to increase taxes in order to finance the temporary increase in spending, tax rates would have to be very high 
3. very high tax rates can lead to very high economic distortions. Faced with very high income tax rates, people will work less or engage in illegal, untaxed activities 
4. rather than moving the tax rate up and down so as to always balance the budget, it is better to maintain a relatively constant tax rate. 
5. tax smoothing implies running large deficits when government spending is exceptionally high and small surpluses the rest of the time.
