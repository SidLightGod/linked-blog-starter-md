1. suppose the government decides to stabilize the debt from year 2 onwards. meaning the debt from year 2 end and thereafter remains at the same level as it was at the end of year 1. 
2. the budget constraint for year 2 is $$ B_2 = (1 + r)B_1 + (G_2 - T_2) $$
3. under our assumption that debt is stabilized in year 2, B2 = B1 = 1 and setting this into the equation we get : $$ 1 = (1 + r) + (G_2 - T_2) $$
4. reorganizing and bringing the $G_2 - T_2$ element to the left side of the equation : $$ T_2 - G_2 = (1 + r) - 1 ==> r $$
5. to avoid a further increase in debt during year 1, the government must run a primary surplus equal to real interest payments on the existing debt. 
6. each year the primary surplus must be sufficient to cover interest payments, leaving the debt level unchanged 

###### this yields our second set of conclusions which are? 
1. the legacy of past deficits is higher than the government debts 
2. to stabilize the debt the government must eliminate the deficit
3. to eliminate the deficit the government must run a primary surplus equal to the interest payments on the existing debt. This requires higher taxes forever.
